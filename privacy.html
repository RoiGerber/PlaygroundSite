Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Playground collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have when you use the Playground app, website, and creator or developer services.

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Who We Are

Playground is a swipe-first gaming app where players discover and instantly play games from creators. In this policy, "Playground," "we," "us," and "our" refer to the Playground app, website, and related services operated by Playground and its developer.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly, such as your email address, contact messages, support requests, and developer program submissions. If you create or use an account, we may collect account identifiers needed to provide the service.

We may also collect device, usage, diagnostics, and gameplay information, including app interactions, games viewed or played, crash reports, performance data, other diagnostic data, IP address, browser type, device type, and similar technical information.

How We Use Information

We use information to operate Playground, launch games, personalize discovery, improve performance, respond to support requests, measure product usage, protect the service, communicate with players and creators, and evaluate developer submissions.

We may use contact information for product updates, developer program communication, marketing, and business inquiries where permitted by law. You can opt out of promotional email by using the unsubscribe instructions in those messages or contacting us.

Analytics, Attribution, and Cookies

Playground may use analytics, attribution, and similar technologies to understand how people use the app and website, measure campaigns, improve the product, and keep the service reliable. These technologies may use cookies, pixels, SDKs, and similar tools.

The app's public release notes mention AppsFlyer integration, which may be used for campaign attribution and app analytics. You can adjust cookie, analytics, and advertising choices through your browser, device, or platform settings where those controls are available.

When We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information in the ordinary sense of exchanging it for money. We may share information with service providers that help us host, analyze, secure, advertise, support, or operate Playground; with app stores and payment or platform partners; with game creators or developer partners as needed to run submitted games; and when required for legal, safety, or security reasons.

If Playground is involved in a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

Data Retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, maintain security, and support legitimate business purposes. Retention periods vary by data type and context.

Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You may also have the right to opt out of certain advertising or sharing activities.

To make a privacy request, contact us at playgroundjune2026@gmail.com. We may ask for information needed to verify and process your request.

Children and Teens

Playground is intended for users who meet the age requirements shown in the app store or platform where they downloaded the app. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us without the required consent, please contact us so we can review and respond.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so please use care when sharing information and protect your account credentials.

International Transfers

Playground and our service providers may process information in the United States, Israel, the European Economic Area, and other locations where we or they operate. Data protection laws may differ from those in your country.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the date above and provide additional notice when required by law.

Contact Us

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to playgroundjune2026@gmail.com.
