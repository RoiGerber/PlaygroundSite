SAFETY

Child Safety Standards

Playground is committed to preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and maintaining a safer experience for players, creators, and the broader community.

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Zero-Tolerance Policy

Playground prohibits any content, behavior, or account activity that depicts, promotes, solicits, facilitates, normalizes, or exploits child sexual abuse or exploitation. We do not allow child sexual abuse material, sexualized content involving minors, grooming, sextortion, trafficking, or attempts to contact minors for sexual purposes.

Content and Account Enforcement

We may remove violating content, restrict features, suspend or terminate accounts, preserve relevant information where legally required or permitted, and report apparent CSAE to appropriate authorities or child-safety organizations.

Reporting Concerns

Anyone can report suspected CSAE, grooming, or child safety concerns involving Playground by contacting playgroundjune2026@gmail.com. Please include enough detail for us to review the concern, such as the username, game, content link, screenshots, dates, or a description of the behavior.

Review and Response

We review child safety reports with priority. Where a report identifies potential CSAE or imminent harm, we may escalate the matter to relevant law enforcement, regulatory bodies, or child protection organizations.

Google Play CSAE Standards

These standards are published externally for app-store compliance, including Google Play's requirements for preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation. For more information about Google Play's CSAE policy, visit the Google Play Help Center.
